Why start at fill-finish and packaging

High consequence. Repeatable inspection.

These late production stages combine costly quality risk with visual tasks and controlled capture conditions that suit a focused computer-vision pilot.

01 · Release risk

High-value batches

A missed defect can trigger batch rejection, recall, compliance pressure, and loss of patient trust.

02 · Visual workload

Repeated checks

Inspectors review particles, fill levels, closures, labels, serial numbers, and dates at production speed.

03 · Controlled setting

Defined inspection

Stable lighting, camera positions, and defect classes support focused datasets and validation.

04 · Quality evidence

Unit-level records

Each result can link its image and decision to a vial, batch, line, and inspection time.

System architecture

Four layers. One connected system.

The concept separates image collection, processing, model inference, and dashboard access while keeping each result connected to its source record.

Connected system layersSelect a layer to explore
LAYER 01 · CAPTURE & PRESERVE

CV input

Fixed or industrial cameras—and supervised operator uploads—capture images. S3-compatible storage such as MinIO can preserve frames, versions, and production metadata.

INPUT & STORAGE
  • Industrial camera
  • Operator upload
  • S3 / MinIO
LAYER 02 · PREPARE & ROUTE

Back-end processing

A FastAPI-style service retrieves images; pre/post-processing and a Celery- or Airflow-style queue route results into structured records.

ORCHESTRATION
  • FastAPI-style service
  • Pre / post-processing
  • Celery / Airflow queue
LAYER 03 · DETECT & CLASSIFY

AI model engine

Pretrained YOLOv8, EfficientNet, or Vision Transformer models can be fine-tuned for defects. Outputs include location, class, confidence, and QA tags; versions can be tracked with MLflow.

VISION & MODEL OPS
  • YOLOv8
  • EfficientNet / ViT
  • MLflow versions
LAYER 04 · REVIEW & REPORT

Dashboard and reports

Production, QA, and management compare line, batch, product, and shift history. Inspection alerts are immediate; aggregate dashboards can refresh every few minutes.

QUALITY INSIGHT
  • Immediate alerts
  • Batch & shift history
  • Periodic dashboards
